# 🔬 Class 10 Science Virtual Lab

> Interactive simulations and concept notes for Class 10 Physics, Chemistry and Biology.

**Live:** https://project-zero-lab.github.io/class10-science-virtual-lab/

**Built by:** Chandan Joshi · Project Zero × Claylab Education Foundation (Cohort 1)

## What it does
- Five interactive experiments aligned with the Class 10 NCERT Science curriculum
- **Physics:** magnetic field of a bar magnet, refraction through a glass slab
- **Chemistry:** electrolysis of water, pH scale and universal indicator
- **Biology:** human heart and blood circulation
- Concept Notes tab for every experiment with key points and laws
- Works on mobile and desktop with touch and drag support

## How to use
1. Open the live link
2. Pick an experiment from the **Select Lab Experiment** menu (☰ on mobile)
3. In the **Simulation** tab, use the buttons and sliders to change the experiment
4. Drag inside the canvas to move the test compass on the magnet experiment
5. Switch to **Concept Notes** to revise the theory
